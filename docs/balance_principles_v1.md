# Balance Principles V1

These rules define the initial testing baseline. They are constraints for implementation and playtesting, not final balance promises.

1. Heroes begin from their source *Heroes of the Storm* statistics and scaling when a conversion brief identifies a source hero.
2. A complete endgame equipment set should generally add 20%–40% total to primary statistics.
3. A complete set must not exceed a 40% total primary-stat increase during the intended initial testing range.
4. That budget applies across the entire set, not independently to every item.
5. Items also provide Armor, so equipped heroes are tougher than naked source Health implies.
6. Tanks may gain effective durability through Armor and defensive effects without reducing source Health.
7. PvE enemies may therefore require stronger statistics than direct HotS Minion, Mercenary, Monster, or Boss equivalents.
8. Difficulty should also come from abilities, compositions, positioning, telegraphs, control resistance, and encounter mechanics rather than raw stat inflation alone.
9. Do not shrink hero numbers merely to compensate for items.
10. Reusable hero mechanics may later become simplified enemy abilities with distinct presentation, timing, and telegraphs.

The complete item catalogue and final PvE scaling tables are outside V1.
