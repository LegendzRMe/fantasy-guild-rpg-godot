# Spirit Form V1

**Lifecycle:** Current contract.

Spirit Form is a controllable death-state window, not a revive or ordinary status buff. Lethal Priest resolution transitions to 1 Health in Spirit Form, clears Shields and temporary Armor, refreshes Basic Ability cooldowns, and lasts eight seconds. The Spirit may move, Basic Attack, and cast Q/W/E; R is blocked. It is Invulnerable, untargetable, excluded from automatic healing selection, and cannot receive normal healing or Shields.

Expiry without Redemption continues into normal incapacitation. Ready Redemption instead clears Spirit Form, preserves the spirit's safe battlefield position, restores 50% maximum Health, and starts its 180-second cooldown. A later death still enters Spirit Form while Redemption cools down, but expiry does not revive.
