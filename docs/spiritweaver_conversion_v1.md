# Spirit Weaver V1 conversion

Spirit Weaver (`spiritweaver`) is a testing-only, resource-free mail Healer inspired mechanically by Rehgar. It keeps a 1900 Health / 3.957 regeneration chassis, 110-damage melee Basic Attack, 0.9-second Basic Action period, 1.5-source-unit attack range, and 4.8398 source movement speed. All scalable flat values use `value * 1.04^(level - 1)`. Mana and Mana talents are absent.

## Source reconciliation

The implementation was checked against the locally expanded current Heroes Tool Chest normalized package `2.55.17.97771`: `data/herodata_97771.json`, `data/unitdata_97771.json`, and `gamestrings/gamestrings_97771_enus.json`. Relevant normalized records include `RehgarChainHeal`, `RehgarLightningShield`, `RehgarEarthbindTotem`, `RehgarPurge`, `RehgarGhostWolfActivate`, `RehgarAncestralHealing`, `RehgarBloodlust`, and the corresponding Rehgar talent links. The package provenance is [Heroes Tool Chest heroes-data2](https://github.com/HeroesToolChest/heroes-data2).

Blizzard's [March 29, 2022 balance notes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23787368/heroes-of-the-storm-balance-patch-notes-march-29-2022) reconcile the current 1900 Health/3.96 regeneration chassis, 60-second Purge, 25% Colossal Totem Health bonus, and 160 Earthliving healing against older values. The [July 15, 2024 PTR notes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24104602/heroes-of-the-storm-ptr-patch-notes-july-15-2024) clarify Chain Heal, Wellspring, and Farseer's Blessing fixes; the [October 14, 2024 live notes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24145596/heroes-of-the-storm-live-patch-notes-october-14-2024) clarify Ghost Wolf presentation and Electric Charge combined healing behavior.

The normalized live package wins where older notes differ. Notable selected live values are: Q 8-second cooldown / 260 heal / two bounces / 7 source-unit bounce range; W 8-second cooldown / 64 damage per second / five seconds / 2.5 radius; E 15-second cooldown / 260 Health / six seconds / 35% Slow; Purge 60 seconds / 0.5-second allied Unstoppable / 80% decaying enemy Slow over two seconds; Ancestral 100 seconds / one-second delay / 1180 healing; Bloodlust 90 seconds / six seconds / 40% Basic Action speed / 35% movement / 30% primary Basic Attack healing. The project brief overrides source mount input with automatic Ghost Wolf and replaces source Healing Totem/Grounded behavior as described below.

## Runtime and input

The contextual Basic Action uses the shared `CombatRulesV1` phase, windup, recovery, and `next_action_ready_time`. An enemy assignment performs the unweakened melee attack; an allied Hero-equivalent assignment performs the established Cleric/Li Li-style 60 Level-1 Basic Heal. A successful Basic Heal makes one 50% bounce to the lowest-Health-percentage second eligible Hero. Attack and heal assignments therefore cannot alternate independent timers. PC dragging and touch dragging can assign an enemy or ally, and both assignments move into their centralized ranges.

Ghost Wolf is source-owned automatic state. Three seconds without a successful personal Basic Action or ability enters Wolf, including while controlled; movement, incoming healing, allied abilities, and incoming damage do not reset it. Wolf grants 20% movement and a 2.25-source-unit lunge; its next enemy Basic Attack adds 60% damage and exits. A personal allied Basic Heal exits without lunge damage. Stun and Silence do not disappear on Wolf entry. The D HUD shows a countdown or `WOLF`, and F3 shows timer, age, cooldowns, relay state, Totem state, stacks, talents, and telemetry.

Q creates an explicit cast ID and owns original target, healed IDs, remaining normal bounces, current origin, and relay usage. Baseline heals three distinct Hero recipients. Blood and Thunder adds one normal recipient; Tidal Waves reduces remaining Q cooldown per Hero healed; Earthliving applies only to manual Q recipients below 50%; Wellspring invokes the same resolver in an explicitly untalented 35% mode. Hunger of the Wolf can use E once as a free unhealed relay, and Spirit Confluence can use Spirit Weaver once as a free healed relay, prevents loops, adds 10% healing inside E, and changes automatic bounces to lowest percentage.

W instances are owned by the casting Spirit Weaver and bearer ID. They tick around the current bearer, follow a repositioned Totem, end if the bearer dies, and have independent Rising Storm stacks. Stormcaller expands radius 25% and grants a Hero bearer +2 encounter maximum Health for each qualifying standard/elite/named/boss/enemy-Hero contact, capped at 200; summons and Totem bearers do not progress it. Electric Charge heals from actual resolved W damage and grants movement while W deals damage. Earth Shield is a timed 40%-maximum-Health Hero shield. Stormbound Fang is one owned damage event equal to five seconds of the instance's current W damage rate; it includes Rising Storm stacks, does not consume W, and does not synthesize Electric Charge ticks.

E is one source-owned, targetable, destructible deployable with Health, lifetime, position, collision radius, and active effects. Recasting replaces the old instance; Colossal Totem instead permits one same-instance reposition preserving Health and remaining duration. Healing Totem has no button: when its 30-second ICD is ready, the next E retains Earthbind behavior, uses E Health, lasts ten seconds, and heals nearby Heroes for 2% maximum Health each second. Grounded sets the total Slow to 45% and permits W on E. Earthgrasp's 90 damage and 90% one-second Slow occur only on initial placement. W ends cleanly after E is destroyed.

Purge uses a single PC/mobile dual-target confirm path. Allied Purge removes supported removable controls, then grants source-owned 0.5-second Unstoppable; Purification heals only after a removal. Enemy Purge removes only effects explicitly tagged both `beneficial` and `dispellable`, applies a control-profile-aware 80% Slow with linear decay over two seconds, removes up to 330 Shield with Purification, and applies 40% healing reduction for two seconds. Cleansing Tempest advances the ordinary 60-second cooldown at 1.5x rate (40 seconds from fully spent), permits self-Purge and only that self-Purge through Stun, and retaliates against successful primary Basic Attack contacts during its actual Unstoppable window. Root does not incorrectly lock an in-range cast; Silence and Fear remain action locks.

Ancestral schedules 1180 healing on another allied Hero after one second. Farseer's Blessing schedules the source-concept follow-up after 1.5 seconds and heals other nearby Heroes for 590. Bloodlust affects nearby Hero-equivalent recipients, excludes disposable summons and E, and grants 40% generic Basic Action speed, 35% movement, and 30% healing from actual primary Basic Attack damage. The generic cadence path means it also speeds a Spirit Weaver Basic Heal, but its life-steal hook never treats that heal as attack damage. Gladiator's War Shout doubles radius and duration.

All 22 choices at Levels 9/12/15/18/21/24/27/30 are centralized in `spiritweaver_data.gd`; Level-27 upgrades enforce their matching Heroic. The three project capstones have stable V1 working names: Stormbound Fang, Spirit Confluence, and Cleansing Tempest.

## Shared architecture, telemetry, and scope

The conversion extends shared target assignment, Basic Action cadence, timed effects, Shield, Unstoppable/control, beneficial-effect dispel metadata, decaying Slow, generic Basic Action speed, projectile/melee primary-Basic-Attack contacts, deployable lookup, safe geometry, source ownership, healing/threat resolution, save migration, presenter, F3, and Testing Range paths. It does not reuse Shaman runtime: Shaman remains a charge/chain-lightning and spirit-summon class, while Spirit Weaver is a contextual-action generalist support with source-owned W/E/Q networks and dual-purpose Purge.

Testing telemetry covers Basic Attack/heal/effective/overheal/bounce, Q recipients/healing/overheal/relays/Tidal/Earthliving, W contacts/damage/Electric/Stormcaller/Rising, E casts/repositions/Slow/damage/destruction/Healing Totem/Wellspring, Purge casts/targets/removals/Purification/Shield/anti-heal, Wolf entry/exit/lunge/bonus, Heroics, Bloodlust recipients/leech, and every G30 trigger. Runtime initializes fresh per encounter; separate Spirit Weavers do not share W, E, Q, Purge, or telemetry state.

Spirit Weaver is present only in the testing save and dedicated Range. No starter, recruitment, campaign, lore, race, faction, or final-art identity was added. Placeholder identity: Kael Runebraid.

## Deliberate deviations and uncertainty

The brief's automatic Ghost Wolf, Healing Totem, Grounded Totem, Hunger of the Wolf, and all three G30 designs deliberately replace source behavior. The source-to-world scalar is the stack convention `185 / 5.5`; collision, lunge landing, cast, bounce, and area geometry remain centralized for tuning. W's 8-source-unit target range and Purge's 7-source-unit target range are the smallest normalized/source-identity-compatible audited choices, but public normalized exports do not provide every engine search/collision scalar in a single canonical record. V1 uses deterministic combat-ID ordering for ordinary Q auto-bounces where the normalized engine search order is not portable; Spirit Confluence explicitly changes that order to lowest Health percentage.

No human interactive playtest is claimed. Manual follow-up remains recommended for attack/heal switching feel, Wolf timing/lunge readability, Q and both relay visuals, W bearer readability, Colossal controls, dual-target/self-Purge on PC and touch, Heroic timing, and class differentiation.
