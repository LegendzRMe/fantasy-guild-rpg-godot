# Paladin (Yrel) mechanics-first conversion V1

Paladin is a resource-free melee Support based on Heroes of the Storm build `2.55.17.97771`. The audited chassis is 2,678 Health, 5.418 Health regeneration, 155 Basic Attack damage, 1.3-second Basic Attack interval, 1.5 source-unit range, and ordinary 1.0 threat. Level scaling uses the project's standard 4% curve. The implementation intentionally preserves Support—not primary-Healer—identity through self-sustain, positional ally buffs, Universal Armor, control, displacement, shielding, and terrain.

Q/W/E use the reusable charged-cast state. A cast starts on button-down, continues through Root, Slow, and Blind, and is cancelled by Stun, Silence, Fear, or forced displacement. A disabling interruption reduces Divine Purpose's current cooldown by three seconds; manual cancellation does not. Cooldowns and Divine Purpose consumption begin only when a cast commits. Because normalized public source data exposes reliable minimum and maximum endpoints but no authoritative continuous damage/healing curve, Vindication and Righteous Hammer deliberately use minimum values below full charge and maximum values at full charge. Avenging Wrath continuously interpolates only travel distance, whose endpoints are authored.

Divine Purpose primes one successful Q/W/E as immediately maximum charged without auto-casting it. Seraphim applies Unstoppable when that empowered cast commits. Vindication damages nearby enemies and heals Paladin. Hammer uses a frontal arc, project-authored knockback, and a maximum-charge Stun. Avenging Wrath uses a blocker-safe leap endpoint and damages/Slows around its landing.

The eight talent tiers are registered through the standard one-choice-per-tier system, including matching Level-27 Heroic upgrades. Shared systems own Universal Armor, Healing Received, outgoing damage reduction, controls, Unstoppable, shields, and the new source-aware Healing Done modifier. Ordinary Hero-equivalent recipients and major companions such as Misha qualify for support; disposable summons do not. Judgment is owner-scoped, is applied after the qualifying maximum-charge damage packet, and amplifies Paladin's later damage only.

Ardent Defender intercepts hostile packets before mitigation, prevents the packet, and converts the audited raw packet amount into healing; this ordering is explicit because the project has no non-mutating damage preview pipeline. Word of Glory copies that conversion to eligible nearby allies. Sacred Ground and Hallowed Ground are documented separately in [sacred_ground_geometry_v1.md](sacred_ground_geometry_v1.md).

The Testing menu exposes Paladin Range with wounded and controlled allies, standard/elite/named/Boss targets, clustered and separated enemies, training targets, and blocker/displacement fixtures. Automated coverage includes charge endpoints, maximum armed state, interruption exclusions, Divine Purpose, representative talents, Healing Done stacking, Sacred ring exceptions, live Q/W/E/R/D resolution, Hallowed relocation, Seraphim, and PC start/confirm behavior.
